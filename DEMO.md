## 🗣️ Persona Voice Lines (pick ONE before recording)

### ⚠️ Whisper + language note

The transcribe API sends a language hint to Whisper based on the UI language. If your UI is set to Kiswahili but you speak English, Whisper may mis-transcribe.

> **Show the Kiswahili UI for ~3 seconds (to prove multilingual), then tap English again before tapping the mic.**

---

### Option A — 🌾 Farming ("Growing food on my family's farm")

**Why pick it:** Most relatable to the "women in SSA" framing. Hits big-name orgs.

| Bloom asks | You say |
|---|---|
| "Hello. I'm Bloom. What do you love doing?" | *"I love growing food on my family's small farm. Especially vegetables like tomatoes and beans."* |
| (likely follow-up about *why*) | *"Because when the harvest is good, my whole family eats well. And I can sell what's left at the market."* |
| (likely about *when it started*) | *"My grandmother taught me when I was a little girl. She always said the soil remembers who loves it."* |
| (if a 4th comes) | *"One day I want to teach other women in my village how to grow more food on less land."* |

**Resulting `/path` cards:** One Acre Fund (Kenya), AGRA, AWARD Fellowships, Heifer International (Zambia).

### Option B — 📚 Teaching ("Teaching village children to read") ⭐ most emotional

**Why pick it:** Strongest emotional pull for judges. Surfaces Camfed (very famous in this space).

### Option C — 💻 Technology ("Building things with computers") ⭐ best for judges

**Why pick it:** Pleasantly subverts the "rural African woman" stereotype. Surfaces **AkiraChix** — a women-in-tech Kenya organization that judges love to see.

| Bloom asks | You say |
|---|---|
| "Hello. I'm Bloom. What do you love doing?" | *"I love building things with computers. I taught myself how to make small websites."* |
| (why) | *"Because I want to build apps that solve real problems for my community. Like helping farmers find buyers."* |

## 🎙️ Recording: live narration + screen at the same time

You **can** record voiceover and screen at the same time in Clipchamp — that's the easier path.

**Setup:**
1. Clipchamp → **Record & create → Screen and camera** → enable the **microphone** too.
2. Open Bloom in one browser window. Open this `DEMO.md` in **Preview** in a second window/monitor.
3. Use your laptop mic for BOTH narration AND for Bloom's voice input (Windows handles this fine).

**Take strategy:**
- Don't try to narrate *during* the actual mic recording with Bloom — let Bloom's TTS reply play.
- Your narration goes in the **gaps between** mic taps:
  - Before tapping mic: *"Now she taps the mic and just speaks…"* → go silent
  - Speak to Bloom (persona line)
  - Bloom replies via TTS → you stay quiet, let it play
  - Tap mic again, answer the follow-up
  - After 3-4 exchanges, narrate again: *"And in a few seconds, Bloom maps her passion to real opportunities…"*

**Expect 2-3 takes minimum.** First take is always rough.

**Test first:** Record a 10-second clip where you both narrate AND tap the mic to talk to Bloom. Play it back. If both voices are clear, you're good.

## 🎞️ Clipchamp production tips

### Pacing fixes
- If your screen recording is too long for the voiceover: select the clip → **Speed → 1.5x or 2x** on the parts without UI changes.
- If you're stuck on a moment: right-click → **Freeze frame** for an extra half-second of breathing room.

### Title cards
- Add a **0.5s pink fade-in** at the start and a **1s fade-out** at the end.
- Use Clipchamp's **Text** tool with the *"Soft"* preset. Font: **Outfit** or **Inter**. Color: `#db2777` (matches Bloom's pink).

### Captions (high-impact, 5 min)
- After recording, click **Use AI captions** in Clipchamp. Style them in bottom-third, white text with subtle drop shadow. Judges often watch with sound off in the first pass — captions can be the difference.

### Background music (optional)
- Clipchamp's free library has acoustic/ambient tracks. Set volume to **15%** so it doesn't fight your voice. Avoid anything beat-heavy.

### Backup plan: AI voice
- If your voice is rough at 11 PM: Clipchamp has **Record & create → Text to speech**. Use *"Nova"* or *"Sonia"* (English-AU sounds warm and trustworthy). Paste the voiceover text in chunks matching each scene.

## ✅ Pre-flight checklist (5 minutes before you record)

- [ ] Bloom is running on `npm run start` (production build, so the offline SW works)
- [ ] Open Edge → DevTools (F12) → device toolbar → iPhone 14 Pro
- [ ] **Application → Storage → Clear site data** so you start fresh (no stale chat)
- [ ] Allow microphone permission once now, so the demo doesn't get interrupted by the prompt
- [ ] Set your system volume to ~60% so the TTS playback records cleanly
- [ ] Close all other tabs, mute Teams/Slack notifications
- [ ] Pick your persona (A / B / C above) and read its lines out loud once
- [ ] Have a glass of water within reach
